# Finora

    College students often struggle with finacial difficulties, many relying on scholarships and/or working a job while attending their university only to just barely scrape by. These students have income is not always stable, and their course load may make it difficult to make time to manage their finances. Finora is a tool to help students keep track of their finacial status by displaying it all in one place in a readable format, while also helping them to plan ahead.
    Finora can help students by tracking and categorizing both regular income and expenses, like jobs and rent, as well as irregular income and expenses like finacial aid and random purchases. Students can view past transactions, with several options for sorting and filtering. It can then display a visual breakdown of the students's spending, and help the student make a monthly budget. Finora can also give the student warnings when they are approaching their budget, helping to prevent overspending in their daily life.
